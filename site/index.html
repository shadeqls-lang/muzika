01 what

what is
muzika.

a soundcloud player for windows, made with two rules: it has to look good, and it has to stay out of the way. it streams soundcloud in small pieces so tracks start almost instantly, keeps your likes without captchas, and goes fully quiet in the tray while you play something else.

05 install

three steps.
that's it.

1

download

one file, muzika.exe. put it in its own folder: muzika keeps everything in muzika_data right next to it, never on c:.

2

open it

windows may say it doesn't know the app. press more info → run anyway. it's open source, you can read every line on github.

3

press link soundcloud and sign in. your likes, playlists and feed show up. updates come by themselves from now on.

06 faq

questions.

is it free?

yes. no ads, no account of its own, no subscription. the code is on github.

is my soundcloud login safe?

muzika signs in through soundcloud's own page and keeps the login encrypted with windows (dpapi) in muzika_data, on your pc only. it is never in backups and never sent anywhere except soundcloud.

why does windows warn me?

the exe isn't signed with a paid certificate, so smartscreen doesn't know it yet. "more info" → "run anyway". github builds every release from the public code.

will it slow down my games?

no. in the tray and behind a game the visualizers and animations stop completely, only the sound keeps going. that's the whole point of muzika.

can i download tracks?

a track page has a download button that saves the stream muzika is already playing. respect the artists: buy and support the ones you love.

how do updates work?

muzika checks once when it starts. a new version downloads on the loading screen and it opens already updated, then shows what's new.
